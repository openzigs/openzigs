3-node Medium Kubernetes Cluster Pricing Comparison
====================================================

Executive Summary
-----------------
This report compares the estimated costs of running a 3-node "medium" Kubernetes cluster on AWS (EKS), Google Cloud (GKE), and Azure (AKS) as of February 2026. It provides a detailed breakdown of compute, storage, control plane, and networking costs for each provider, with both hourly and monthly totals. Discounts, spot/preemptible options, and key pricing assumptions are noted, with direct links to all sources.

Assumptions
-----------
- Each cluster has 3 "medium" worker nodes (see per-provider specs below)
- Each node has 100GB of block storage
- 100GB/month outbound data egress (first 100GB is free for AWS/Azure, charged for GCP)
- Control plane pricing as per standard managed Kubernetes offerings
- On-demand pricing unless otherwise noted; discounts and spot/preemptible options discussed separately
- 730 hours/month used for monthly calculations
- All prices in USD, as of Feb 2026


AWS EKS (us-east-1)
-------------------
- **Worker Nodes:** 3 × m5.large (2 vCPU, 8GB RAM) — $0.10/hr each ([source](https://cloudprice.net/aws/ec2/instances/m5.large))
- **Storage:** 3 × 100GB EBS gp3 — $0.08/GB/month ([source](https://aws.amazon.com/ebs/pricing/))
- **Control Plane:** $0.10/hr per cluster ([source](https://aws.amazon.com/eks/pricing/))
- **Networking:** 100GB/month egress — $0 (first 100GB free) ([source](https://aws.amazon.com/ec2/pricing/on-demand/))

Discounts & Spot/Preemptible Options
------------------------------------
- **AWS:** Reserved Instances/Savings Plans (up to ~40% off EC2), Spot (up to 70-80% off EC2, interruption risk)
- **GCP:** Committed use (up to 57% off), Preemptible VMs (up to 80% off VM cost)
- **Azure:** Reserved VM (up to 40–72% off), Azure Hybrid Benefit (up to 40% off), Spot VMs (up to 80% off)
- Storage and control plane costs are generally not discounted by spot/preemptible options

Notes
-----
- All prices are estimates and may vary by region, usage, and provider updates. Always use the official pricing calculators for precise quotes.
- Discounts require commitments or bring-your-own-license (BYOL) eligibility.
- Spot/preemptible/low-priority VMs offer large savings but can be interrupted at any time.
- Control plane pricing and SLAs differ by provider and tier.
- Egress costs can be significant if usage exceeds free tier or 100GB/month.
