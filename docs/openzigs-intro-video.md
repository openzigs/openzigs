# OpenZigs — Intro Video Manuscript

> **Format:** 60-second product intro
> **Tone:** Direct, confident, technically grounded
> **Pacing:** One idea per scene. Let the visuals breathe.

---

## Scene 1 — The Problem

A developer sits at a glowing terminal surrounded by dozens of open browser tabs. AI chat windows, API dashboards, social media panels, video editing timelines — all separate, all disconnected. The look on their face: exhausted.

**Narration:**
Your AI assistant can answer questions. But it can't ship a video. Post to Instagram. Generate music. Monitor your systems. And orchestrate five agents at once, while asking for your approval before doing anything dangerous.

Until now.

---

## Scene 2 — Introducing OpenZigs

The chaotic screen dissolves into a single, clean dark interface. A chat input. A streaming response. Tools firing off in the sidebar. The developer leans back and smiles.

**Narration:**
OpenZigs is an open-source AI automation platform built on GitHub Copilot. One agent loop that can read files, browse the web, write code, generate images, synthesize speech, compose music, and publish to every social platform you use.

---

## Scene 3 — The Agent Loop

An animated diagram shows a user prompt entering the system. The agent thinks, selects a tool, executes, evaluates the result, and selects the next tool — a clean recursive loop. An approval badge glows amber before a high-risk action fires.

**Narration:**
The agent loop is agentic by design. It doesn't take orders — it takes goals. If a file is missing, it finds it. If a step fails, it routes around it. And before anything risky happens, it asks you first.

---

## Scene 4 — Director Mode

A markdown document labeled "Blog Post" flows into a pipeline. Scenes generate. Voiceover synthesizes. Music composes. A timeline assembles in real time. A finished video renders out. The whole pipeline takes under two minutes.

**Narration:**
Director Mode turns any script into a produced video. Write your narration. Choose your voice. The system generates scene visuals, synthesizes speech with F5 TTS, lays in original AI music, and renders a finished video, without you touching a timeline.

---

## Scene 5 — Social Brain

A unified inbox fills with Instagram comments, Twitter replies, LinkedIn DMs, and YouTube notifications — all converging into one thread. Rules fire automatically. Posts schedule. A team member gets a handoff notification.

**Narration:**
Social Brain unifies every platform into one intelligent inbox. AI handles the routine. You handle the relationships. Schedule posts, auto-respond to comments, and escalate anything that needs a human — across Instagram, Twitter, YouTube, LinkedIn, and more.

---

## Scene 6 — Safety & Control

The approval queue appears: a list of pending agent actions. Shell command. File write. Social post. The user reviews each one, approves, and the agent continues. Nothing ships without a green light.

**Narration:**
Power without guardrails isn't useful — it's dangerous. Every high-risk tool requires your explicit approval. You define what the agent can do autonomously, and what it waits for. You stay in control, always.

---

## Scene 7 — Open Source, Local First

A macOS terminal shows a `brew install` command completing in seconds. The app opens. A GitHub repo page in the background shows thousands of stars. Code is visible, auditable, yours.

**Narration:**
OpenZigs is free, open source, and runs entirely on your machine. No subscriptions. No vendor lock-in. Your data stays local. And because it's built on open standards, you can extend it to do anything.

---

## Scene 8 — Call to Action

The OpenZigs logo fades in over a dark background. Clean. Minimal. Confident.

**Narration:**
OpenZigs. The AI platform that actually does the work.

Get started at openzigs.com. Or install it right now with Homebrew.

---

*End of manuscript. Total estimated runtime: ~60 seconds.*
